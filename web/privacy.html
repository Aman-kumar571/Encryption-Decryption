Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Local Client-Side Processing

FileCrypt does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. When using the browser tool, all encryption and decryption operations are performed entirely within your browser using the standard Web Crypto API. Your files and passwords never leave your device.

The desktop application runs completely offline and makes zero network requests.
